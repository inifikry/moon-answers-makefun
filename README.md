# The Moon Finally Answers Back

One line, in the voice of the Moon replying at last to everyone who has ever
stared up at it, wished on it, howled at it, and made it late-night promises.

> You wish on me every night. I'm a rock. I don't grant wishes. I just sit here, glowing, judging your life choices in silence.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: no network, no
randomness, no clock. The line is a fixed constant in `main.py`; the same
text is kept in `line.txt`.
